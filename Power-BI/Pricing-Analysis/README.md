# Medical Equipment Partner Pricing Compliance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer revenue and contract pricing to evaluate whether customers are being appropriately aligned with their contracted pricing tiers.

The goal of the analysis was to identify differences between contracted pricing and actual customer revenue performance and provide a data-driven approach for evaluating pricing tier placement.

> **Note:** This portfolio project is based on skills and analytical methods used during an internship project. Company names, customer information, financial figures, and other proprietary information have been removed or replaced to protect confidential information.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The business needed a way to evaluate customer performance across different contract pricing tiers.

The original approach used performance ranges to categorize customers. The analysis was redesigned to use percentile-based segmentation so that customers could be evaluated relative to the overall customer population.

The analysis focused on:

* Customer revenue
* Contract pricing
* Pricing tiers
* Customer performance
* Percentile segmentation
* Opportunities for pricing optimization

## 📈 Analysis

### Revenue Calculation

Monthly customer revenue was calculated from the available revenue data.

### Percentile Segmentation

Customers were ranked based on their revenue performance and segmented using percentile thresholds.

The analysis focused on the **80th and 90th percentiles** to create performance groups rather than dividing customers into equal-sized thirds.

This resulted in four performance categories that could be used to evaluate customers relative to the overall population.

### Power BI

Power BI was used to create measures, analyze customer performance, and visualize the results.

DAX was used to calculate revenue metrics and customer rankings.

## 💡 Business Value

The analysis provides a framework for identifying customers whose pricing tier may not align with their observed revenue performance.

Using percentile-based segmentation provides a more data-driven method of evaluating customer groups and can help support future pricing and contract decisions.

---

## 📊 Project Outcome

The final analysis provided a Power BI-based approach for evaluating customer performance and pricing tier alignment.

The project also demonstrated how an existing business analysis can be redesigned when business requirements change, including modifying the analytical methodology from simple performance ranges to percentile-based segmentation.
